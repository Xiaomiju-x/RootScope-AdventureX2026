# Results and Truth Boundaries

## Verified in the controlled demonstration

- Four fixed physical cards were recognized in the event setup.
- Three abstract downward probe levels were physically qualified.
- The grass-card chain completed twice: visual recognition, level-1 descent,
  five-second irrigation, pump-off and locked final state.
- The public kiosk and website expose evidence but do not grant remote control.
- The X5 loaded the local Fast/Deep/BM25 explanation paths in read-only mode.
- Two independent BPU replay paths completed 43/43 cases.

## Statements that require qualification

- “4K” describes the camera capability; the stable live demo used 1080p30.
- Probe step counts are mechanical tiers, not centimeters or biological root
  depth.
- LLM inference is on CPU, not BPU.
- BPU was qualified on-device but is not the current actuator authority.
- The current probe does not automatically return upward.
- A protocol acknowledgment is not by itself proof of visible water delivery.

## Not claimed

- arbitrary wild-plant recognition;
- autonomous multi-cycle mechanics without human reset;
- validated water-saving percentage;
- production agricultural reliability;
- cloud-to-actuator control;
- a fully released/reproducible hardware stack.
